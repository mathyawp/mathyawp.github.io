How to use this
The idea. On each interval the oscillation Mi − mi is multiplied by the weight Δαi. The gap is the sum of those products — and in the lower box it is literally an area, because the widths Δαi add up to 1.

Two ways to win. Make the oscillation small (a continuous f on short intervals), or make the weight small (α barely moves there). Either shrinks a term. Try Only the middle and watch the outer intervals contribute nothing at all.

Refinement. Drag Intervals, or press Split largest contribution to cut the worst interval in half. Adding points can only lower U and raise L, so the gap never grows.

Where it fails. Choose Dirichlet f. Every nondegenerate interval holds both rationals and irrationals, so Mi − mi = 1 no matter how fine P gets. The shaded area stays the whole box: the gap is stuck at 1 and f is not integrable.

Common refinement. Turn it on to replace P by P ∪ Q. Rudin's lemma says the common refinement is at least as good as either partition — the gap you read can only drop.
