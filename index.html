Francis Su

I teach, explore, and write about the beauty and joy of mathematics and the dignity of human beings. My work explores what math is, who it is for, and how good teaching can help people find more belonging in it.

I'm the Benediktsson-Karwa Professor of Mathematics at Harvey Mudd College, a former president of the Mathematical Association of America, and the author of Mathematics for Human Flourishing.

AN ENCOURAGEMENT

Yes, you are a math person.

I hope the work gathered here helps you see aspects of yourself in mathematics, not because math is impressive or useful, but because math is bound up with deeply human desires—for play, beauty, truth, justice, freedom, and community. Those desires are already part of you, and they can awaken the mathematical nature within.

Believe that you and everyone in your life can flourish in mathematics.

Francis Su

Author, teacher, and mathematical explorer.
