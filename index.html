AN ENCOURAGEMENT

Yes, you are a math person.

I write for a wide audience — especially for those who don't see themselves as "math people." — to help them see math as intimately tied to being human. Our deepest human desires reveal our mathematical nature, and it only needs to be awakened.

Believe that you and everyone in your life can flourish in mathematics.
