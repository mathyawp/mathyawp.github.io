Research

The mathematics of shape and fairness

My research is a mixture of geometry, topology, and combinatorics — you can think of these as the mathematics of shape, stretching, and counting, respectively. I often study problems motivated by the social sciences, and my work intersects game theory and the mathematical modeling of decision-making.
